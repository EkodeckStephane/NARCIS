\opening{Dear Editors,}

We submit the research article entitled \textbf{ARCIS: Authenticated Robust Cover-Selection Image Signaling under Finite-Index Constraints} for consideration in \emph{ACM Transactions on Multimedia Computing, Communications, and Applications}.

ARCIS studies selection-based coverless image communication as a finite-index authenticated signaling problem. Its contribution couples a calibration-locked codebook, complementary five-cover group banks, a keyed cyclic mapping with Gray-indexed symbol order, authenticated framing, replay control, Reed--Solomon correction, and an explicit feasibility test for complete protected messages. Standard cryptographic and coding primitives are used as components; the scientific contribution is the integrated finite-index construction and its frozen external evaluation.

The experimental study separates development from external validation. The operating point ($K=8$, five covers per coded symbol, RS128) was established on BOSSBase and then frozen for five seeded train--index resamplings of Caltech-101. Within each resampling, the 1,500-image descriptor-training set and 7,000-image cover index are disjoint; across seeds, the index pools overlap because all five runs resample the same 9,144-image corpus. External calibration recovered 1,800/1,800 authenticated messages, and eight held-out attack parameterizations from the same four attack families yielded 1,163/1,200 recoveries (96.92\%). A controlled Reed--Solomon study quantified the reliability--communication tradeoff, increasing recovery from 69.08\% at RS0 to 96.92\% at RS128 while mean transmitted images per session increased from 970 to 2,676.7. The paired trial analysis contains 334 RS0-to-RS128 gains and zero losses. At RS128, 8-, 32-, and 64-byte payloads require 2,320, 2,640, and 3,070 images. The selection-detector audit uses 20 image-disjoint train/test repetitions within each Caltech resampling. The five resampling means are reported descriptively, rather than as independent replications, and yield mean macro-AUC 0.504, 0.513, and 0.527 for residual, texture, and CNN wardens, with resampling ranges 0.5014--0.5052, 0.5120--0.5138, and 0.5232--0.5301, respectively.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved the submitted version. The author order is Arthur Ulrich Ewane; Juvet Karnel Sadié; Stéphane Gaël R. Ekodeck; Serge Alain Ebélé; Vivien Loïck Beyala Kamgang; Damaris Belle M. Fotso; and Chantal Marguerite Mveh-Abia. Chantal Marguerite Mveh-Abia is the corresponding author.

We believe the work fits TOMM through its combination of multimedia representation, robust image communication, authenticated signaling, selection-security evaluation, and reproducible empirical analysis. The manuscript also quantifies the operational traffic cost of the robust operating point and places recent cover-selection work in its native recovery semantics.
